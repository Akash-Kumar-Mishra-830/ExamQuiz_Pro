Your data & privacy

Profile, quiz progress, rewards aur reviews is browser ke local storage mein rakhe jaate hain; app inhe kisi account server par upload nahi karta. Reviews mein naam aur mobile number rating ko pehchanne/update karne ke liye locally rakhe jaate hain; public review card par mobile number nahi dikhaya jata. Logout profile details hata deta hai, lekin saved reviews aur quiz progress isi browser mein reh sakte hain.
